# Year 1 --- Foundational Governance & Protection

## Objective

Establish the people, governance, identity, endpoint, asset,
risk-management and incident-response capabilities required before
advanced monitoring.

## Team

-   Cybersecurity / GRC Manager
-   GRC & Risk Analyst
-   Security Engineer / IAM Engineer
-   Security Analyst

**Estimated Year 1 personnel budget: approximately \$600K.**

## Priority Areas

-   Governance and policy
-   Cybersecurity risk management
-   Risk appetite and tolerance
-   Business impact analysis
-   Asset and software inventory
-   Third-party governance
-   Security ownership
-   Identity and access management
-   Endpoint protection
-   Foundational incident response

## Microsoft Foundation

The plan proposes **Microsoft 365 Business Premium at approximately
\$22/user/month** for 250 users, or about **\$66K/year**, subject to
final licensing validation.

Use Microsoft Entra ID, Intune and Defender to support centralized
identity, MFA, Conditional Access, device management and endpoint
protection.

## Year 1 Exit Criteria

Oscorp should demonstrate defined governance, an active risk process,
improved inventory, centralized IAM, managed endpoints, foundational
incident response and documented third-party security processes.
