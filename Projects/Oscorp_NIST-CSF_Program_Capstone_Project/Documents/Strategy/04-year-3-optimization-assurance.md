# Year 3 - Optimization, Assurance & Audit

## Objective

Shift from implementation toward evidence-based assurance.

## Priorities

-   Continuous control monitoring
-   Automated evidence collection
-   Penetration testing
-   Vulnerability assessment and management
-   Cloud posture management
-   Privileged-access maturity
-   Third-party reassessment
-   Incident-response drills
-   Recovery exercises
-   Security metrics and KRIs
-   Internal audit/control-effectiveness testing
-   Remediation verification

## Cloud Security

Evaluate **Microsoft Defender for Cloud** as Azure workloads expand.
Foundational CSPM supports assessments, recommendations, asset inventory
and compliance visibility. Paid capabilities should be justified by risk
and workload requirements.

## Vulnerability Management

The source plan uses a planning assumption of approximately
**\$2/user/month**, or roughly **\$6K/year for 250 users**, for
Microsoft Defender Vulnerability Management, subject to final
product/licensing validation.

## Exit Criteria

Controls should be documented, owned, monitored, tested, evidenced,
reassessed and improved.
